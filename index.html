01 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of vision and medicine.

My work develops AI systems that move beyond static visual recognition toward fine-grained perception, evidence-grounded clinical reasoning, and intervention-aware prediction.

My research journey spans small-lesion segmentation, cross-modal and surgical video reasoning, and generative world models for ophthalmic procedures. I aim to make medical AI more reliable, interpretable, and clinically meaningful.

I am pursuing a joint PhD at the University of Nottingham and Shenzhen University (expected February 2027), supervised by Prof. Linlin Shen, Dr. Jianfeng Ren, and Prof. Rong Qu.
