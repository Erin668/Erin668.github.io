About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the joint doctoral programme between the University of Nottingham and Shenzhen University. I am very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Linlin Shen, Dr. Jianfeng Ren, and Prof. Rong Qu.

My research focuses on multimodal medical AI, medical image and video understanding, evidence-grounded clinical reasoning, and generative world models. I am interested in learning reliable representations from heterogeneous medical evidence, reasoning across clinical modalities, and predicting intervention-dependent future states.

I have four accepted first-author publications, including two CVPR 2026 papers, AAAI 2025, and Pattern Recognition.

Research Interests

  • Fine-Grained Medical Image Understanding: Small lesion segmentation, multimodal fusion, semantic anchoring and robust visual representations.
  • Multimodal Clinical Reasoning: Medical vision-language models, ophthalmic diagnosis, evidence-grounded reasoning and spatiotemporal surgical video understanding.
  • Generative World Models: Intervention–response modeling, tissue dynamics and future surgical-state prediction.
