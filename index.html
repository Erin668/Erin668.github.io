About Me

Research biography

I am a PhD candidate in the joint doctoral programme between the University of Nottingham and Shenzhen University, expecting to graduate in February 2027. My supervisors include Prof. Linlin Shen, Dr. Jianfeng Ren, and Prof. Rong Qu.

My research spans medical image and video understanding, multimodal clinical reasoning, and generative world models. I am especially interested in how AI systems ground their decisions in visual evidence, reason across modalities, and anticipate the consequences of interventions.

I have first-author papers at CVPR 2026 (two papers), AAAI 2025, and Pattern Recognition. I am currently exploring evidence-centered clinical AI and world modeling for ophthalmic surgery.
