About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the joint doctoral programme between the University of Nottingham and Shenzhen University, expected to graduate in February 2027. I am supervised by Prof. Linlin Shen, Dr. Jianfeng Ren, and Prof. Rong Qu.

My research focuses on multimodal medical AI, medical image and video understanding, evidence-grounded clinical reasoning, and generative world models. I seek to develop models that learn reliable visual representations, reason from heterogeneous clinical evidence, and predict intervention-dependent future states.

My first-author publications include two papers at CVPR 2026, one at AAAI 2025, and one in Pattern Recognition. My ongoing research spans medical multimodal reasoning and world modeling for ophthalmic surgery.

Research Interests

  • Fine-Grained Medical Image Understanding: Small lesion segmentation, multimodal fusion, semantic anchoring and reliable representation learning.
  • Multimodal Clinical Reasoning: Medical vision-language models, evidence-grounded reasoning, ophthalmic diagnosis, and spatiotemporal understanding of surgical videos.
  • Generative World Models: Intervention–response modeling, tissue dynamics and future-state prediction in ophthalmic surgery.
